## Measurement model (what the numbers mean)

- **host_receive** = CLOCK_MONOTONIC at completed-packet detection in the
  libfreenect2 stream parser (fork stamp) — the closest observable to "USB
  capture callback"; excludes only in-flight USB transfer time.
- **delivery latency** = listener delivery − host_receive; includes depth
  processing / JPEG decode + async queue.
- **assembled latency** = pairing instant − earliest host_receive of the
  pair; the E1 "available to a would-be renderer" number.
- **skew** = color device timestamp − depth device timestamp of the pair,
  in device clock (ticks of 0.125 ms, confirmed).

## The 15 Hz color finding (the one open condition)

Discovery 01 flagged "color halves to 15 Hz under low light" as
[verify → E1]. **Confirmed on this unit, with mechanism:** sensor-reported
exposure sat at 60.4–66.3 (its "covered/dark" end of the 0.5–60+ scale),
and color inter-arrival locked to 66.66 ms exactly — the documented
auto-exposure integration-time doubling, not a throughput limit. Depth USB
bandwidth, OpenCL processing, decode, and the tee all held 30 Hz
concurrently, so nothing machine-side blocks 30 Hz color.

**Closed 2026-07-30 (Caden present, room lights on).** Four runs:

| Run | Exposure control | Color rate | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `out-lit` 30 s | auto | **14.97 Hz** | exposure *still pegged at 66.47 ms* — warm room lighting is below the sensor's 30 Hz threshold; the frame is correctly exposed but firmware doubles integration |
| `out-lit-manual` 20 s | `setColorManualExposure(33 ms, gain 3)` | 14.85 Hz | integration provably applied (JPEG metadata reads 32.99 ms) yet readout stayed halved — manual integration alone does not restore rate |
| `out-lit-manual15` 20 s | manual 15 ms | 14.95 Hz | same: rate is not a pure function of integration time in manual mode |
| `out-lit-semi` 20 s | `setColorSemiAutoExposure(16 ms)` | **29.85 Hz** ✅ | the semi-auto command pins pseudo-exposure below the 16.667 ms readout boundary → full 30 Hz; image is near-black in this room (16 ms of dim warm light) |

**Conclusion.** 30 Hz color is achievable on demand via
`setColorSemiAutoExposure(<16.7 ms)`; whether the resulting image is
*usable* is purely a question of scene illumination. In Caden's actual
room lighting the honest operating point is: 15 Hz well-exposed color
(fine — the frame contract pairs latest-fresh color by design and the
assembler already reports `color_age_ms`), switching to semi-auto 30 Hz
only when the scene is lit for it (e.g. a real shoot with lights).
Wiring this as a `KinectSource::Config` option is deliberately deferred
until a color-critical look needs it.

## IR-at-silhouette (grounds 04 §1's confidence-proxy [verify])

Across 4 depth+IR snapshot pairs (static chair scene): IR median at
depth-discontinuity pixels ≈ 720–780 vs interior ≈ 1930; 52.9–54.8 % of
edge pixels fall below the interior 25th percentile. **IR intensity does
dim at discontinuities** — usable as a brightness-mapped confidence proxy
— but separation is moderate, not crisp, and this scene had no moving
person. Luminous Shell may map it to a glowing fringe as designed;
claiming it *isolates* flying pixels specifically remains unproven until a
moving-subject take exists.

## Timestamp semantics (now measured, for the frame contract)

- Device ticks are 0.125 ms; both streams share one device clock domain.
- Color−depth skew is a *constant* −10.875 ms (p95 |skew| 11.0 ms, max
  11.0) — stable enough to treat as a calibration constant, re-measured
  per session.
- No wrap handling needed within a session (32-bit ticks wrap ≈ 6.2 days).

## Consequences for the foundation

- OpenCL depth processor is the production path until/unless CUDA is
  installed (see docs/build.md); CPU depth is diagnostic-only.
- Tee pool of 8 is generous; keep 8 (67 MB) for headroom.
- Frame contract gets `t_host_receive` from the fork stamp; pairing may
  assume the constant skew model but must re-measure it live.
- Recorder planning rate: ~13 MB/s depth (u16 mm) + ~21 MB/s color at
  30 Hz + IR optional — E3's synthetic rates match this.
