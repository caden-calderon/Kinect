# E2 — Motion-Field Source Quality and Cost

**Status: PASS — closed 2026-07-30 with the seated-swing clip. Production
flow term: DIS FAST (3.4 ms p50, body-mask FB coverage 0.77 mean during
fast swings). ULTRAFAST fails the quality bar on fast motion and is
demoted to a degradation-ladder/preview tier. See "Quality results".**

Run 2026-07-29 on the E1 dim clip (60 frames, static chair scene, 1080p
JPEG → grayscale 512×424). Spike: `spikes/e2-flow-probe/dis_probe.py`.
CPU: this workstation (same session as E1; environment in E1.md).

- **OpenCV CUDA is unavailable**: Arch's `opencv 5.0.0` package is built
  without CUDA modules (`cv2.cuda.getCudaEnabledDeviceCount() == 0`), so
  the `cuda::FarnebackOpticalFlow` probe cannot run without a custom
  OpenCV build. Given DIS ULTRAFAST/FAST land at 1–4 ms on CPU, a custom
  CUDA OpenCV build is not worth its maintenance cost unless the quality
  pass fails DIS entirely.
- FB-consistency machinery validated (96–99% coverage on the static
  scene — trivially high, as expected with ~0.1 px median flow; **not** a
  quality verdict).

## Quality results (closed 2026-07-30, seated fast-swing clip)

Clip: `out-clip-body`, 887 color frames at 30 Hz (Caden seated ~1 m,
pose hold → fast arm swings → out-of-frame excursion → close lean).
Analysis: swing window = top-quartile motion-energy frames (222 frames);
"body mask" operationalized as pixels with |flow| > 1.5 px (the room is
static, so movers ≡ the body; median mask ~55 k px ≈ 25 % of the raster).
Script: `spikes/e2-flow-probe/e2_quality.py`.

| Preset | Body FB-coverage mean | p10 | frames ≥ 0.70 | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DIS ULTRAFAST | 0.633 | 0.367 | 41 % | ❌ fails on fast motion |
| DIS FAST | **0.766** | 0.485 | **73 %** | ✅ meets the ≥ 0.70 bar |

Gross-direction check: flow overlays rendered at the 8 highest-motion
frames; fields are spatially coherent, confined to the body, and match
the visible motion (vertical field on a raise, oblique field mid-swing;
static background clean). Judged by visual inspection of the overlays,
not annotation tooling — adequate for a gross-direction criterion.

## Consequence for the motion-field design (phase 4)

- **DIS FAST is the flow term** (3.4/4.2 ms p50/p95 — well inside the
  8 ms budget). Preset stays a parameter; ULTRAFAST remains available as
  a degradation rung and preview mode, with the measured caveat that its
  vectors break up on fast limbs (coverage p10 0.37).
- Even FAST's p10 is 0.49: during the very fastest swings, up to half the
  body's vectors fail round-trip consistency. The phase-4 fusion design
  should treat FB-consistency as a per-pixel confidence input (down-weight
  inconsistent vectors toward the finite-difference/skeleton fallback)
  rather than trusting raw flow everywhere.
